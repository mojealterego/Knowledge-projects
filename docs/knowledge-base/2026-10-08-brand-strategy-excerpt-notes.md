## New P66/P34 abstraction
```
CUSTOMER EVIDENCE + COMPETITOR ALTERNATIVES
 → GUIDING PRINCIPLE → COST/DIFFERENTIATION HYPOTHESIS
 → BRAND POSITIONING → RESOURCE/DELIVERY CAPABILITIES
 → PRICING + MARGIN ESTIMATE → CUSTOMER EXPERIMENT
 → REAL OUTCOME / REVISION
```

A positioning hypothesis requires independent evidence, not rhetorical novelty. `BrandPositioningExperiment` should bind `customer_segment`, `guiding_principle`, `differentiating_capability`, `competitor_set`, `offer`, `pricing_assumption`, `source_refs` and `result_state`. Profit and market advantage remain unverified until measured. No fabricated testimonials, deceptive advertising or exploitation of vulnerability.
