## 1. Archaeometric evidence and Late Bronze Age collapse
`Upadek Epoki Brązu- Klimat i Cywilizacje.pdf` has **two** strands: (a) Voynich parchment and pigments and (b) eastern Mediterranean collapse. The source mentions AMS parchment 1404–1438 CE, chemical analysis, pollen/speleothem proxies, drought event ~3.2 ka BP, archaeoseismology, Ugarit letters, Sea Peoples and trade-network fragility.

**Crucial correction:** material dating measures when animal parchment originated, not directly the inscription or authorship date. The source proposes causal synergy between climate, earthquakes, conflict, migration and trade; chronological coincidence does **not** establish independent causal attribution. Radiocarbon calibrations, earthquake timing, eclipse associations and texts require primary-source verification. P19 should use this as a claim/measurement/causal-inference evaluation fixture with explicit dissent/counterexamples; no novel "historical oracle" product.

## 2. Persona research and negotiation corpora
Two `Unikalna Osobowość` versions (~96.96% normalized similarity) discuss activation/persona vectors, Big Five, MBTI, Cicero/Diplomacy, negotiation, prosocial datasets, Hermetic corpora, QLoRA/SFT and model-merging. **A persona is statistically exhibited response style, not consciousness or tool authorization.** MBTI and hermetic content are not substitutes for scientifically validated personality measurement or user consent. Dataset licenses, terms, PII, social manipulation, stereotyping and safety regression matter. P91/P11 should empirically compare base/prompt/adapted models on held-out tests; no model retraining here.

## 3. MLIR/Omnis/CIRA and OmniCore MVP
The 7-page legacy report describes MLIR multi-dialect IR, region/GC/ownership concepts, heterogeneous lowering and CIRA verification. The 16-page OmniCore report contains illustrative Rust `no_std`, a supervisor scheduler, AI Foundry generator, GDScript UI and Android fragments, plus speculative TND/Hilbert-based concepts.
**Design boundary:** generated kernel code is not automatically bootable or memory-safe; no probabilistic AI is granted Ring-0 actuation. Confirm Rust target, bootloader, build, memory, interrupts, QEMU, ABI, device safety and measured performance. "MLIR makes all languages equivalent" is a thesis, not proof; `CiraMigrationProof` and differential testing are required. P80/P87/P23 are the existing owners.

P11 integrates benchmarking; P17/P27 receive the policy/evidence contracts.
