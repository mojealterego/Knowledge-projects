## Security and scientific controls
Analyze manipulative **design patterns** without inferring a specific viewer's mental state or susceptibility. Hypnotic/ACC/priming assertions in the source are hypotheses or context-dependent findings, not reliable coercion techniques. No induced flash/strobe experiments or optimization for vulnerable audiences.

## Verification
Source-backed disclosure check, flashing/motion review against applicable accessibility standards, alt-text/keyboard audit, opt-out parity, promotion-vs-organic separation, independent evaluation of high-risk creative prompts.
